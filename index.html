Our Story

Born on volcano ridges before first light

Dirt and Dawn started with four friends running the trails outside Bandung every Sunday at 4:30 — headlamps, hand-me-down packs and a stubborn love of the dark. We built the gear we couldn't find: honest kit, made for humidity and hard ground, priced so the entry fee never outruns the trail fee.
