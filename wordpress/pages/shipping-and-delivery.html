Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how Dirt Dawn ships orders placed at dirtdawn.solutions.

1. Processing Time

Orders are prepared and handed to the carrier within 1–2 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays). Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.

2. Shipping Destinations and Rates

We currently ship within the United States. Shipping rates and any free-shipping threshold are shown at checkout before you pay.

3. Delivery Time

Delivery takes roughly 3–7 business days after your order ships, depending on the carrier and destination. Busy periods and weather can cause delays; we appreciate your patience.

4. Tracking

You receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. If tracking has not updated within 7 business days, contact us and we will look into it.

5. Address Errors

Please double-check your address at checkout. If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we can reship it at your cost. Contact us as soon as possible if you spot a mistake — we can usually correct it before the order ships.

6. Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package arrives damaged or goes missing, email us within 14 days of the delivery date (or of the last tracking update) with your order number and a photo if applicable, and we will make it right.
