Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Dirt Dawn uses cookies at dirtdawn.solutions.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. We also use similar technologies such as local storage. They help the site work, remember your preferences and understand how it is used.

2. Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — required for the cart, checkout and security. The site cannot function without them.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices such as language and region.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand which pages are visited so we can improve the Site. Data is aggregated.
  • Session cookies — keep you signed in while you browse and expire when you close your browser.

3. Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you disable essential cookies, parts of the store — such as the cart and checkout — will not work.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some embedded content or payment tools may set their own cookies. We keep third-party cookies to the minimum needed to run the store.

5. Changes to This Policy

We post any updates on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
