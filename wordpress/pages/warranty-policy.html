Warranty Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Dirt Dawn gear is built to last. This policy explains the limited warranty that covers products bought at dirtdawn.solutions.

1. One-Year Limited Warranty

Dirt Dawn warrants its products against defects in materials and workmanship for one (1) year from the date of delivery, when used for their intended purpose. If a product fails because of a manufacturing defect within that period, we repair or replace it — or, if it is no longer available, refund you.

2. What Is Not Covered

  • Normal wear and tear (outsoles, straps, fabric pilling, fading).
  • Damage from misuse, improper washing, accidents or modification.
  • Items bought from anyone other than Dirt Dawn or its authorised sellers.

3. Making a Warranty Claim

  • Email dirtndawn@hotmail.com with your order number, a description of the problem, and photos showing the defect.
  • If the claim is approved, we explain the repair, replacement or refund and any next steps.

This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights that vary from state to state.
