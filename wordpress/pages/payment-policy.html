Payment Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how payment works when you shop at dirtdawn.solutions.

1. Accepted Payment Methods

We accept major credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Available payment methods are shown at checkout and may vary by region.

2. When You Are Charged

Your card is authorised and charged when you place the order. If an item is unavailable and we cancel that part of your order, the corresponding amount is refunded to your original payment method.

3. Currency and Taxes

All charges are in U.S. dollars (USD). Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address and applicable law.

4. Payment Security

Checkout is encrypted over HTTPS and processed by our payment provider. Dirt Dawn never sees or stores your full card number. Your card statement will show the charge as "Dirt Dawn" (or a close variant we display at checkout).

5. Failed or Declined Payments

If a payment is declined, no order is created and you are not charged. Check your card details and billing address, or try another card. Contact your bank if the problem continues.

6. Pricing Errors

If a product is listed at an incorrect price, we may cancel the affected order and refund you in full, even after a confirmation email was sent.
