Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains when and how you can cancel an order placed at dirtdawn.solutions.

1. Cancelling Before Shipment

You may cancel an order for a full refund as long as it has not yet shipped. Email dirtndawn@hotmail.com as soon as possible with your order number and the subject line "Cancel order #..." — we process cancellations within 1 business day.

2. Cancelling After Shipment

Once an order has shipped we cannot stop the carrier. Refuse the delivery if you are present, or accept it and use our Returns & Refunds Policy to send it back for a refund.

3. Cancellations by Us

We may cancel an order if an item is out of stock, the price was wrong, or the payment cannot be verified. You receive a full refund to the original payment method and an email explaining what happened.

4. Refund Timing

Approved cancellations are refunded within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.
