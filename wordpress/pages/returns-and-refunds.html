Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

If something is not right, we want to fix it. This policy explains how returns and refunds work at dirtdawn.solutions.

1. Return Window

You may return unworn, unwashed items with original tags within 30 days of delivery for a refund to your original payment method.

2. How to Start a Return

  • Email dirtndawn@hotmail.com with your order number and the item(s) you want to return.
  • We reply with return instructions and the return address.
  • Ship the items back; we recommend a trackable service.

3. Refunds

Once your return arrives and passes inspection, we refund the item price (plus any applicable tax) to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is our fault.

4. Defective or Wrong Items

If an item arrives damaged, defective or not the one you ordered, tell us within 14 days of delivery with a photo and your order number. We cover return shipping and offer a replacement or full refund.

5. Exclusions

Items marked "final sale" and gift cards cannot be returned unless defective.

6. Exchanges

For a different size or colour, return the original item and place a new order — that is the fastest way to get the size you want.
