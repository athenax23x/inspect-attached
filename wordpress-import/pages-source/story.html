Our Story

Built before first light

Dirt and Dawn exists for the early starts: the headlamp miles, the cold coffee, the long climb that earns the sunrise. We make outdoor and trail-running gear for people who would rather be on the trail when the day begins than anywhere else.

Our approach is simple. Every piece in the range — from a featherweight merino tee to a 12-litre race vest — is designed around real use on hard ground and in changing weather, with details that earn their place and nothing that doesn’t. And it is priced so the entry fee never outruns the trail fee.

We are an independent brand based in Oxon Hill, Maryland, shipping internationally. As the range grows, so does the standard we hold it to: honest kit, made for movement, ready before first light.
